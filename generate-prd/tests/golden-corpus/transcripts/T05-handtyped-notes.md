# Looper feedback — internal sync notes — 2026-04-29

Quick notes from the Tuesday customer-advisory hour. Five attendees, mostly mid-market ops leaders. Not a structured interview — just what came up.

## Top of mind

- onboarding still the #1 complaint
- "I had to call support twice in the first hour" — direct quote from Bluebird Logistics
- new admins don't know where to start; no clear "next step" on the empty workspace
- looper invite flow sends three separate emails per user — confusing
- workflow speed mentioned by 2 of 5; not as hot as onboarding but trending

## Specific friction points

- CSV import — column header rules not surfaced until validation fails
- SSO setup buried under a "developer" menu — non-technical admins miss it entirely
- empty-state on dashboard is blank, no suggested actions
- no in-app tour; help docs open in new tab and people lose context
- "I felt like Looper assumed I already knew how to use Looper" — Bluebird again

## Asks / wishlist

- guided onboarding wizard for first 24 hours after signup
- in-product checklist ("invite team, import shipments, run first route")
- video walkthrough on the empty dashboard
- one-click sample data so new users can poke around before importing real data

## Pricing color (not the focus today)

- 3 of 5 said pricing was "fine" at current tier
- 1 said they'd want volume discounts past 100 seats
- 1 (Riverstone, sole operator) repeated the free-tier ask — known position

## Comp / lost deal note

- lost Helios to legacy filemaker thing — compliance was the cited reason, not product fit
- onboarding came up there too — they said our trial onboarding scared their dispatcher pool
